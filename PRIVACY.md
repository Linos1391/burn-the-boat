# Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

## What we collect

When you sign in with Google (OAuth) or with email and password, we store your email address and, for Google sign-in, your display name and profile picture. We also store the content you create in the app (plans, pairings, boat requests, stakes and transactions).

## How we use it

Your email is used only to identify your account, authenticate you, verify your email address and operate app features. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Third parties

Authentication and storage are handled by Supabase and Google. Payments are processed by PayPal; we do not store your card or PayPal credentials.

## Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in Settings, which removes your stored email and data. Inactive guest accounts are deleted automatically.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue at https://github.com/Linos1391/burn-the-boat/issues or email me by linos.coding@gmail.com.
